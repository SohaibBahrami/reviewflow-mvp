# Product brief

## Customer

Solo video editors who spend too much time chasing client feedback.

## Pain

Feedback arrives through WhatsApp, Telegram, email, Google Drive comments, voice notes, and vague messages like “the middle feels weird.” The editor then has to reconstruct what the client meant and where it applies.

## Promise

**One link. Timestamped feedback. Clear approval.**

## Differentiation

We should not compete with enterprise review platforms on feature count. We should win on simplicity for solo editors:

- client opens a link without learning a new system
- feedback feels like a conversation, not a proofing workflow
- every comment is attached to a timestamp
- unresolved feedback becomes an obvious revision list
- approval is explicit and records the exact version

## Example

Client watches a 4-minute YouTube video and leaves:

- 00:14 — “Make this opening faster.”
- 01:22 — “Please remove this shot.”
- 02:51 — “This music is too loud.”

The editor sees three concrete tasks tied to the timeline, then uploads v2 and asks for approval.
